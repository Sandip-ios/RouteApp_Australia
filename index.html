      Welcome

      Plan, optimise, deliver

      DropLine puts your delivery stops in the fastest order and guides you with spoken directions. Here's how it works in 30 seconds.

      Create a route

      Make one route for each run or day. Tap ⋮ on a route to rename it, copy it for tomorrow's run, or delete it.

      Add your stops

      Scan run sheet to photograph the paper manifest page by page, Import manifest for a CSV file, or Add stop and type a few letters of the address.

      Step 3

      Optimise & prepare

      Tap Optimise route for the fastest order from where you are. In Details, tap Prepare for no signal before leaving the depot.

      Optimise routeFit map
      Prepare for no signalDirections and map saved for the whole run
      Step 4

      Deliver

      Tap Navigate for turn-by-turn voice directions. Mark each stop Delivered, Failed or Skipped and the next one slides in. Tap a stop to add notes or mark a pickup.

      Mark as DeliveredMark as FailedMark as Skipped
      Last step

      Allow access

      DropLine uses your location to start routes from where you are, and can let you know when a download or your run is finished.
