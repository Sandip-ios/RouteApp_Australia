Getting started

  • Create route on the home screen and give it a name.
  • Add stops: type an address, Scan run sheet (photo of the paper manifest) or Import manifest (CSV file or pasted list).
  • Tap Optimise route for the fastest order from where you are.
  • Tap Navigate for turn-by-turn directions, or open the stop in Google Maps, Waze or Apple Maps.
  • Mark each stop Delivered, Failed or Skip. When every stop is done you get a run report you can share.

Common questions

Does it work without mobile signal? Yes. Addresses for your area and your route stops are saved on the phone automatically. Before leaving the depot, open the route's Details tab and tap Prepare for no signal to save directions and the map.

Which areas are covered? All of Australia, using the official national address list (G-NAF).

Do I need an account? No. Your routes stay on your phone. Use Share route file to move a route to another phone.

Does it use live traffic? Not yet. It is planned for a later version.

How do I move a route to a new phone? Open the route, tap ⋯ → Share route file, send it to the new phone, then on the home screen tap Open a route file.
