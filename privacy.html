Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026 · Dropline route planner, made by Enterprise Analytic

In short: Dropline keeps your routes, addresses and notes on your phone. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and we do not sell or share personal information. To plan routes and show maps it sends locations and searched addresses to map and routing services, described below.

Who we are

Dropline is made by Enterprise Analytic. Contact us at sandip@enterpriseanalytic.com about anything in this policy.

What stays on your phone

  • Routes and stops: delivery addresses, parcel numbers and sizes, notes, pickup/delivery type, and delivered/failed/skipped status. They are stored only in the app's storage on your phone. Delete a route in the app, or uninstall the app, to remove them.
  • Run sheet photos: photos you scan are read on the phone itself (Google ML Kit on Android, Apple Vision on iPhone). The photo is not uploaded, and it is not kept after you finish scanning.
  • Address data: official address lists for your state may be downloaded and saved on the phone so search works without signal. This is public data, not information about you.
  • Directions and map pictures saved with "Prepare for no signal".
  • Settings: your chosen states, notification preference and similar choices.

Location

With your permission, Dropline uses your phone's location while the app is open to start routes from where you are, to suggest nearby addresses first, and for turn-by-turn navigation. Your location is not stored on any server by us. When you plan or navigate a route, your current position is sent to the routing service (see below) to calculate directions.

Information sent to other services

To work, the app contacts these services. Each receives your device's internet (IP) address as part of any internet request, and they handle it under their own policies:

  • Routing (OSRM public server, Project OSRM / FOSSGIS): the map coordinates of your start and stops, to calculate travel times, the best order and turn-by-turn directions. Street addresses and customer names are not sent, only coordinates.
  • Address search (Photon by Komoot, and Nominatim by the OpenStreetMap Foundation): the address text you type or import, only when it is not found in the official address list already in the app.
  • Map pictures (OpenStreetMap tile servers): requests for the map areas you view or save.
  • App and data hosting (GitHub Pages, GitHub Inc.): the app itself and the official address files are downloaded from GitHub's servers.
  • Code and fonts (Cloudflare cdnjs and Google Fonts): map software and the app's fonts are downloaded from these networks.

If you email us (Contact or Report a bug), we receive your email address and what you write. A bug report adds technical details you can see before sending: app version, phone model and system, screen size, permission status, chosen states and the number of stops in the open route. It does not include addresses or customer details.

Camera and notifications

The camera is used only when you scan a run sheet. Notifications are created by the app on your phone (for example "Run finished"); no notification server is used, and you can turn them off in the app's Settings or in your phone's Settings.

Customer information

Run sheets contain your customers' addresses. You are responsible for handling them under your employer's or client's rules. Dropline keeps them only on your phone and sends only map coordinates for routing, as described above.

Children

Dropline is a work tool for delivery drivers and is not intended for children.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn off location, camera or notifications at any time in your phone's Settings.
  • Delete routes in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored.
  • Under the Australian Privacy Principles you can ask what personal information we hold about you (normally only emails you have sent us) and ask us to correct or delete it. Email us; if you are not satisfied with our response you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Future versions of Dropline may add optional accounts or route sharing; this policy will be updated before such features are released.
