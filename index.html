    What works in v1

    Fully working (needs internet)

    • Australian address search with suggestions
    • Up to 25 stops, numbered on the map and in the list
    • Drag-and-drop or ▲▼ reordering
    • Route optimisation of every pending stop together, using real road travel times
    • Road route line, total drive time and distance
    • Pending, delivered, failed (with reason) and skipped statuses
    • Auto-save and resume on this device, named copies, export/import file

    Demonstration only

    • Free public map services: no guaranteed uptime, light testing only, no live traffic
    • If the routing service is down, times fall back to straight-line estimates (marked "Estimate")
    • "Open in Google Maps" hands each stop to the Google Maps app, with directions from the previous stop (or the start)
    • The 25 test stops are public places, not real customers

    Not built yet

    • Built-in voice turn-by-turn navigation and automatic rerouting (planned for the Android app)
    • Delivery time windows, parcel sizes, multiple drivers
    • Accounts, cloud sync and business dashboard
    • Proof of delivery (photo or signature)
